# Numerical source map

- Frozen selection baselines: `baselines.json.methods`; original-arm and sensitivity scores: `rescore.json.frozen_summary` and `rescore.json.variants`.
- Original table field mapping: `tables.json.sources`; the rendered cells in `paper/tables.tex` are generated by `research.replay.render_tables`.
- Follow-up T7: `results.json.consistency`; T8: `results.json.ladder`; T11: `results.json.metamorphic`.
- Follow-up macros and tables: `paper/followup-facts.tex` and `paper/followup-tables.tex`, derived from those same fields. Local source counterpart: `followup-results.json` in the experiment archive.
- Follow-up source selections, partitions and labels: `results.json.ladder_cases`, `ladder_observations`, `metamorphic.rows`, `metamorphic_source_splits`, `reference_reuse` and `design`.
- Local source paths and hashes: `results.json.sources`. These are archive references, not files bundled with this publication.
- Original narrative macros: `paper/facts.tex`, retained unchanged in content from the archived replay and checked locally by `python -m research.replay --check`.
- Public numerical aggregation checks: the two `test_public_paper_*` tests in `tests/test_decision_contract_protocol.py`.

Published checks start from derived outcomes. Raw-response parsing and geometric scoring require the separately retained local archive.
